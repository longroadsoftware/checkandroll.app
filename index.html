How it works

  1. Pick your rig and its list

    Travel trailer, fifth wheel, motorhome, toad or tow vehicle: each has its own built-in checklists, ready to go.

  2. Go step by step

    Check or skip each one in order. A step that depends on an earlier one waits its turn.

  3. Critical steps ask you to confirm

    The black tank valve and other critical steps need a confirm, not just a tap. Skip one, and the banner turns red so you don't drive off and miss it.

  4. Done is green

    Finish every step, and the banner turns green and says so.

Hands-free, if you like

Say the word, and Check & Roll reads each step aloud and listens for “done,” so your hands stay on the job. It works with Siri, and with no signal, anywhere you park.

Questions

What is it?

Check & Roll is an iPhone and iPad checklist app for RVers. It walks you through setup, departure and maintenance in the right order, with no account and no Long Road Software server.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account and no Long Road Software server. Your checklists stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Can my partner and I share a list?

Yes. You can invite your partner, or anyone else, to a checklist through iCloud. Starting a share needs Pro; the people you invite join free, and everyone can check off steps.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time unlock, with Family Sharing, not a subscription.
