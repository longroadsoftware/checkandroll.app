Adding, changing, reordering and deleting steps

Every built-in checklist can be adjusted to match your rig, and you can write your own lists too.

Open the editor

From a checklist, open Edit Checklist.

Add a step

Type it in the Add a step field at the bottom of the list. For a step with a note, a location, or steps it has to wait on, use Add Step with Details from the More menu instead.

Change a step

Tap any step to open it. There you can change its wording, mark it critical (and write the question the hands-free walk-through asks for it), say where it's done, and choose which earlier steps it has to wait on.

Reorder steps

Tap Reorder, then drag a step to its new place. Or touch and hold a step for Move Up and Move Down.

Delete a step

Swipe a step left to delete it. Undo brings it back until you save the checklist.

Notes on steps

Check & Roll Pro lets you add your own note to a step, like where a valve hides. Notes that came with a built-in checklist are always shown, even without Pro.
