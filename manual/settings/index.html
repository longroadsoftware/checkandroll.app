Settings

A quick tour of everything in Settings.

What's in Settings

  • Your name — shown in run history on other devices, so everyone can see who checked what.
  • Rigs — rename, add or delete the RVs and vehicles you keep checklists for.
  • Voice — the hands-free walk-through's wake word, speaking speed, and chimes and haptics.
  • iCloud — whether your devices are in step, and Sync Now. See iCloud and sharing a checklist.
  • Shared checklists — once a list is shared, see who's in it, remove someone or stop sharing. To share a list, use Share Checklist in the checklist's More menu.
  • Notifications — whether you're told when someone else finishes a shared checklist.
  • Pro — what Check & Roll Pro unlocks, and Restore Purchases.
  • Siri & Shortcuts — the phrases Siri already knows, and how to add your own through the Shortcuts app. See The hands-free walk-through and Siri.
  • Privacy — a plain-words summary of what the app does and doesn't send anywhere. See the full privacy policy.
  • Feedback — report a problem or suggest a feature, if you're using a test copy of the app.
  • About — the app's version, and Help and Support.

More help

Help and Support, in Settings › About, opens this website. See Support for how to get help, or the rest of this manual for how to use the app.
