Rigs and checklists

Add your rig, then start one of its built-in checklists.

Add a rig

Open Check & Roll and add your first rig from the Home screen. Choose its type — travel trailer, fifth wheel, motorhome, toad or tow vehicle — and it comes with its own built-in checklists, ready to use.

Start a checklist

Tap a rig to see its checklists, and tap one to open it.

The built-in lists are a starting point. Check them against your owner's manual, and change anything that doesn't match your rig — see Adding, changing, reordering and deleting steps.
