iCloud and sharing a checklist

Keep your rigs and checklists in step between your own iPhone and iPad, or share one with a partner — all through your own iCloud account.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Check & Roll keeps your rigs, checklists and run history in step across your devices, using the private part of your own iCloud account. Long Road Software can't see it.

In Settings › iCloud, see whether your devices are in step, and tap Sync Now.

Sharing a checklist

Invite your partner, or anyone else, to a checklist through iCloud: open the checklist and choose Share Checklist from its More menu. Starting a share needs Check & Roll Pro; everyone you invite joins free, and everyone can check off steps.

You decide who's invited. To see who's in a shared list, remove someone or stop sharing, tap the list in Settings › Shared checklists.

For what's kept and shown when you share a list, see the privacy policy.
